Dr Anupama has been conferred the <b>ECSI Young Scientist Award 2026</b> by the Electrochemical Society of India (https://www.ecsi.org.in/index.html, https://www.linkedin.com/company/electrochemicalsoceityofindia/) in recognition of my contributions to electrochemical interfaces, energy materials, and sustainable electrochemical systems. 

The award was presented on 28th August 2026 during the National Symposium on Electrochemical Science and Technology (NSEST 2026), held at the Indian Institute of Science, Bengaluru, with the ceremony held at the J. N. Tata Auditorium.
